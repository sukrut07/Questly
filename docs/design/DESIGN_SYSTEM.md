**Design System:** Questly Neo-Brutalist & Gamified Productivity  
**Visual Reference:** Matiks (`https://matiks.com/`)  
**Status:** Run 1 Complete — Design System Extracted & Specified  

---

## 1. Design Philosophy

Questly 2.0 fuses the visceral excitement of competitive brain-training gaming platforms with the structured clarity of developer productivity tools.

Key pillars:
1. **Neo-Brutalist Restraint**: Crisp 1px borders, bold condensed display headers, high-contrast action buttons, and purposeful grid alignments without excessive blur, glassmorphism, or distracting gradients.
2. **Tactile Interaction**: Spring-based micro-interactions providing immediate haptic and visual feedback on touch.
3. **Cognitive Clarity**: Information-dense dashboards with clear visual weight, ensuring primary actions (starting a focus session, completing a quest, taking notes) are reachable in under 2 taps.
4. **Category Identity**: Distinctive, harmonious accent colors (Cyber Blue, Emerald Green, Tangerine Orange, Soft Lavender, Bubblegum Pink, Electric Lime) delineating life areas.
